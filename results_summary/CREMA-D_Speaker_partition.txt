CREMA-D Speaker 客户端划分说明（论文写作素材）
CREMA-D Speaker-based Client Partitioning (Notes for Paper Writing)
================================================================

一、任务与问题背景 / Task and Motivation
----------------------------------------------------------------
CREMA-D 的情感分类在本项目中采用 HAP-vs-rest 二分类口径：happy 为正类，
其余 5 类情感合并为负类，正类占比约 17%。受保护属性为种族（Caucasian / 非
Caucasian）。

The CREMA-D task in this work is formulated as HAP-vs-rest binary
classification: "happy" is the positive class and the remaining five emotions
are merged into the negative class, giving a positive rate of roughly 17%.
The protected attribute is race (Caucasian vs. non-Caucasian).

若沿用标签 Dirichlet 划分（按类别比例把样本分配到客户端），在 α=0.1 时大量
客户端只能拿到负类样本，客户端本地标签分布严重偏斜；全局模型在 0.5 阈值下会
退化为多数类预测器（ACC≈0.829、DEO=0、SPD=0），所有算法逐位相同，实验失去
区分度。

Under label-Dirichlet partitioning (samples distributed across clients
according to class proportions), α=0.1 leaves many clients with negative
samples only. The resulting client-local label distributions are severely
skewed, and the global model collapses to a majority-class predictor at the
0.5 threshold (ACC≈0.829, DEO=0, SPD=0), making all algorithms numerically
identical and the comparison uninformative.

二、Speaker 划分的定义 / Definition of Speaker Partitioning
----------------------------------------------------------------
我们把划分单位从"样本"改为"说话人"：同一说话人的全部录音整体分配给同一个
客户端，任何说话人不会同时出现在两个客户端中。

We change the unit of partitioning from samples to speakers: all utterances
of a speaker are assigned to exactly one client, so no speaker appears in more
than one client.

具体步骤（见 module/partition.py 的 _speaker_client_assignment 与
_speaker_indices）：

1. 从数据集读取每个样本的 speaker_id（CREMA-D 共 91 位说话人）。
2. 取训练集与测试集说话人的并集，按固定种子（base_seed=42）用 PCG64 打乱。
3. 打乱后的说话人按轮询（round-robin）依次分配到 client 0 … K−1，因此各客户端
   持有的说话人数最多相差 1，K=20 时每个客户端分到 4–5 位说话人。
4. 该分配同时作用于训练集与测试集：先对并集分配一次，再分别映射回两个划分的
   样本下标。因此某客户端的本地训练数据与本地测试数据来自同一批说话人，而
   训练集与测试集之间的说话人本身互不重叠。

Concretely (see _speaker_client_assignment and _speaker_indices in
module/partition.py):

1. Read the speaker_id of every sample from the dataset (CREMA-D has 91
   speakers in total).
2. Take the union of speakers in the training and test splits, and permute
   them with a PCG64 generator seeded by base_seed=42.
3. Deal the permuted speakers round-robin to clients 0 … K−1, so client sizes
   differ by at most one speaker; with K=20 each client holds 4–5 speakers.
4. Draw the assignment once over the union and map it back to both splits, so
   a client's local training and local test data come from the same speakers,
   while the training and test speaker sets remain disjoint.

三、为什么这样划分是合理的 / Why This Partitioning Is Appropriate
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. 每个说话人在 CREMA-D 中都录制了全部 6 种情感，因此按说话人划分后，每个
   客户端都自然保留完整的标签分布，不会出现"只有负类"的退化客户端。
2. 同一说话人的声音特征（音色、基频、语速）不会跨客户端泄漏，避免了说话人
   身份带来的信息泄漏，比按样本随机划分更严格。
3. 训练集与测试集说话人互斥，测试指标衡量的是对未见说话人的泛化，而不是对
   同一说话人其他语句的拟合。
4. 该协议与 FedAudio（ICASSP 2023）对 CREMA-D 的划分方式一致，便于与已有
   工作直接对比。

1. Every CREMA-D speaker covers all six emotions, so after speaker-based
   partitioning each client naturally retains the full label distribution and
   no client degenerates to a single class.
2. Voice characteristics of a speaker (timbre, pitch, speaking rate) cannot
   leak across clients, which is stricter than random sample-level splitting.
3. Training and test speakers are disjoint, so the reported metrics measure
   generalization to unseen speakers rather than fitting other utterances of
   the same speaker.
4. The protocol follows the speaker-based partitioning used by FedAudio
   (ICASSP 2023) for CREMA-D, which makes the comparison with prior work
   direct.

四、与标签 Dirichlet 划分的区别 / Difference from Label-Dirichlet
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 划分依据：Dirichlet 按"标签比例"切分样本，α 控制客户端之间的类别倾斜程度；
  Speaker 按"身份"切分，谁说的音频归谁。
- 客户端同质性：Dirichlet 下客户端标签分布差异由 α 决定，α 越小越异构；
  Speaker 下各客户端的标签分布近似一致，异构性体现在说话人音色与录音条件。
- 适用性：Dirichlet 适合考察标签分布偏移下的公平性；对 CREMA-D 这种正类仅
  17% 的二分类任务，α 较小时会产生病态客户端，因此不适合作为该数据集的主
  实验口径。

- Splitting criterion: Dirichlet splits samples by class proportions, with α
  controlling the degree of label skew; Speaker splits by identity, assigning
  each speaker's audio to one client.
- Client homogeneity: under Dirichlet, label distributions across clients are
  controlled by α and become more heterogeneous as α decreases; under Speaker,
  label distributions are approximately uniform across clients, and the
  heterogeneity lies in speaker timbre and recording conditions.
- Applicability: Dirichlet is suitable for studying fairness under label
  distribution shift. For CREMA-D, whose positive rate is only 17%, small α
  produces pathological clients, so it is not appropriate as the main
  protocol for this dataset.

五、实现与超参 / Implementation and Hyperparameters
----------------------------------------------------------------
- 代码位置：module/partition.py
  - _speaker_client_assignment：说话人 → 客户端 的分配
  - _speaker_indices：按分配把样本下标分桶
  - partitioner 标识：speaker_v1（写入分区指纹，跨版本可复现）
- 划分种子：base_seed = 42；三次 repeat 复用同一划分，repeat 只扰动客户端采样、
  模型初始化与 dataloader 顺序，从而排除划分方差、保证配对比较。

实验配置（与本项目其他 CREMA-D 结果文档统一口径）：

  任务            AUD_CLF，HAP-vs-rest 二分类（HAP=1，其余 5 类=0）
  数据            CREMA-D，7442 条音频、91 位演员
  敏感属性        种族（Caucasian=1，非 Caucasian=0；Race=Unknown 的演员整体丢弃）
  模型            AUDIOCNNRNN
  客户端划分      Speaker（speaker_v1）
  客户端数        K = 20
  通信轮数        I = 200
  本地 epoch      T = 2
  batch size      64（有效 batch 256，梯度累积 4）
  优化器          Adam，lr = 1e-3
  repeat          3（Mean±STD）
  结果目录        result_path/CREMA-D/Speaker/<algorithm>/AUDIOCNNRNN/20Clients/

Experimental setup (aligned with the other CREMA-D result documents):

  Task            AUD_CLF, HAP-vs-rest binary classification (HAP=1, others=0)
  Data            CREMA-D, 7,442 clips, 91 actors
  Protected attr. Race (Caucasian=1, non-Caucasian=0; actors with Race=Unknown dropped)
  Model           AUDIOCNNRNN
  Partition       Speaker (speaker_v1)
  Clients         K = 20
  Rounds          I = 200
  Local epochs    T = 2
  Batch size      64 (effective batch 256 via 4-step gradient accumulation)
  Optimizer       Adam, lr = 1e-3
  Repeats         3 (Mean±STD)
  Result path     result_path/CREMA-D/Speaker/<algorithm>/AUDIOCNNRNN/20Clients/

- Code: module/partition.py
  - _speaker_client_assignment: speaker-to-client assignment
  - _speaker_indices: bucket sample indices by the assignment
  - partitioner tag: speaker_v1 (included in the partition fingerprint)
- Partition seed: base_seed = 42. All repeats reuse the same partition; repeats
  only perturb client sampling, model initialization and dataloader order, so
  partition variance is removed and paired comparisons are exact.
- K = 20; I = 200; T = 2; batch = 64; Adam, lr = 1e-3.
- Result path: result_path/CREMA-D/Speaker/<algorithm>/AUDIOCNNRNN/20Clients/

六、可直接引用的表述 / Reusable Sentence
----------------------------------------------------------------
中文：
"考虑到 CREMA-D 的正类占比仅约 17%，标签 Dirichlet 划分会使部分客户端仅包含
负类样本并导致模型退化为多数类预测器，我们遵循 FedAudio 的协议按说话人划分
客户端：同一说话人的全部录音归属同一客户端，训练集与测试集说话人互不重叠，
91 位说话人以固定种子打乱后轮询分配到 20 个客户端。"

English:
"Since CREMA-D has a positive rate of only about 17%, label-Dirichlet
partitioning leaves some clients with negative samples only and drives the
model to a majority-class predictor. Following the FedAudio protocol, we
therefore partition clients by speaker: all utterances of a speaker belong to
one client, training and test speakers are disjoint, and the 91 speakers are
shuffled with a fixed seed and dealt round-robin to 20 clients."
